New Student Pipeline (Notion)

Live snapshot of the New Student Scheduling database - the team's true intake workload. The Notion schema uses four statuses (Not started, In progress, Hold, Done); nearly every new request starts in "In progress" so the meaningful capacity signals are age of the in-flight records and how many are past the 10-day SLO. Active = Not started + In progress + Hold, read from Status rather than from Date Completed (about 29% of finished records, all pre-2026, never got a completion date).

Why open records are waiting

14 of 16 open records have no Delay cause recorded. Until that field is filled in, this dashboard cannot separate a slow coordinator from an unresponsive family or a genuine lack of instructor availability — and aging alone will read as a coordinator problem whether or not it is one. Requiring a Delay cause on anything past 10 days would close that gap.
